`merged` colour tokens for a merged pull request, GitHub's merged purple on this palette: `merged`, `merged-foreground` and `merged-background` (light and dark), built as the status colours are, on new `violet` 400, 600 and 700 primitives. Utilities `text-merged`, `bg-merged`, `text-merged-foreground` and `bg-merged-background`.
